Welcome

I'm Justin, N9HO — a ham radio operator with a passion for low-power portable operations, CW (Morse code), and sharing the hobby with others.

This site is where I document field activations, share equipment reviews, and help newcomers discover the joy of Morse code. Whether you're a seasoned operator or just getting started, I hope you find something useful here.

When I'm not on the air, you'll find me testing new gear, building antennas, or planning my next POTA activation.
