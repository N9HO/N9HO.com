Brass Knuckle Node

On the air

Portable, straight to the key. Most of my QSOs happen from a picnic table with a wire in a tree. POTA activations are the excuse; the hunt for a clean, quick CW exchange is the point.

QRP is a challenge, not a religion. I run real power when the band or the pileup calls for it, but there is nothing like getting through on a few watts. When I turn it down, it is for the fun of it.

2 m CW is criminally under-used. That is why I run with the Brass Knuckle Gang, below, and why the skimmer node exists: so 144 MHz contacts show up on the RBN like HF ones do.

New to CW? Call me anyway. Slow is fine. I will match your speed, send a proper roger, and QRS as far as you need. Everybody was 5 WPM once.

Brass Knuckle Gang

A law-abiding, FCC-respecting gang for operators who pound brass on 2 meter CW. It started in northern Utah in 2025 and now has members across the country and a few overseas. I was an early member, number 32, and I am the OG for the state of Alabama: the first brass pounder here, and the one to work if you want in.

The name is literal. Pounding brass is working a manual key. The gang exists because 144 MHz CW is nearly empty and the people who use it are some of the best operators you will meet.

Want in? Work an existing member on 2 m CW, send them the UTC time and date, and you get a number. If you are in Alabama, that member is probably me. Watch for me on POTA and SOTA activations and bring a key.

Learn CW

Tap a character to hear it the way you should learn it: as a sound, at speed, never as a picture of dots and dashes.

The mistake almost everyone makes is memorizing a chart and counting dits. It works up to about 10 WPM and then falls apart, because at real speeds there is no time to count. The fix is to only ever hear characters fast, with long gaps between them while you are learning. That is the Koch method with Farnsworth spacing, and it is how Another Morse Trainer works.

Start with two characters. Add one when you copy 90%. Learn your callsign, a question mark, and your state, and you have enough to hunt POTA.
