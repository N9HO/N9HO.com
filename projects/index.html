Projects

Every one of these started as something I needed at my own station or in my own field kit.

The three big ones first, then the smaller tools I built for my own shack. Some have a write-up that shows how they work.

Another Morse Trainer

Learn CW by ear with the Koch method. Characters at 33 WPM from day one, recognition time tracked on every answer, and 22 ways to train, from confusion drills to a simulated pileup. Free and open source, on iPhone and Android. The live CW decoder inside it is Jay's, W6JV.

Carrier Wave

A logging suite for the field. Log a POTA or SOTA activation on your iPhone, spot yourself with one tap, and have the log upload itself to pota.app and QRZ when you pack up. Co-founded with Jay, W6JV.

Brass Knuckle Node

An inexpensive 2 m CW skimmer that feeds the Reverse Beacon Network and survives sitting next to an HF amplifier. Filter first, LNA second, surge pigtail as the only DIY part. Roughly $150 to $200 per node. Modeled, not yet built.

  • Design & build guide
  • Brass Knuckle Gang

QRZ Setup Photo
During a POTA activation I send a photo of my setup from my iPhone and it shows up on my QRZ page. A banner above it flickers ON AIR while POTA has a spot for me, then goes dim and shows my last park when I am off air. A small Python app on a home server does the work and strips the GPS data from the photo first. It works, and it is still getting field time.
